Unit 1: Introduction to Artificial Intelligence
Topics include AI fundamentals, machine learning, deep learning, natural language processing, and generative AI.

Unit 2: Prompt Engineering
Topics include prompt design, zero-shot prompting, few-shot prompting, temperature, and prompt constraints.

Unit 3: Large Language Models
Topics include transformers, tokenization, autoregressive generation, text generation, and question answering.

Unit 4: Retrieval Augmented Generation
Topics include document loading, text chunking, embeddings, vector databases, retrieval, and generation.
